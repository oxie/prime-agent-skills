## Evidence to collect

User need, supporting components, dependencies, qualitative evolution judgments and any evidence-backed movement hypothesis.

## Geometry and reading order

Vertical axis runs visible to user at top to less visible below. Horizontal bands are Genesis, Custom-built, Product and Commodity. Put components at judged positions; connect dependencies with thin lines. Optional evolution arrows point right and are labeled as forecasts when uncertain.

## Honesty and review

Positions are strategic judgments, not measured percentages or deployment coordinates. Do not invent movement or equate commodity with universally best. Keep straight dependency lines when elbows would suggest different positions. Distinguish hypothetical future movement from a verified past shift.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
