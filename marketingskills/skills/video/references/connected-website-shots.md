# Connected generated shots for website assets

Use for a requested related clip family or a still-to-motion website handoff.
Start with Image's [connected asset workflow](../../image/references/website-asset-workflow.md)
and its [worked pack](../../image/references/website-asset-pack/index.md); do not
restart intake. That pack supplies placement, reference roles and approved visual
constants. This reference owns temporal decisions, not the website framework.

- Distinguish source identity from motion direction. A reference can fix the
  subject without dictating camera movement; an action reference must not import
  an unapproved person, product or voice. Inspect permissions before uploads.
- Plan motion that the available source views support. Record shot start/end,
  carried state, camera direction and meaningful transitions. Obtain missing
  product views rather than presenting invented geometry as real.
- Assemble shared constants plus the selected shot and its explicit overrides
  into a usable prompt. Duration comes from the approved content and verified
  model contract. Continuity is a testable intent, not guaranteed by wording.
- Keep source asset, prompt, model/settings, submitted job, chosen take and final
  encoded derivative associated in existing project state. After a source or
  narration change, recheck dependent clips/captions; old completion must not
  survive merely because the shot name is unchanged.
- Separate generation time from delivered timeline time. Trims, overlaps, holds
  and scroll mapping affect the result. A single continuous film with chapters
  needs shared-film time ranges, not the full film repeated for every chapter.
- Inspect rendered motion and final files. Adjacent stills alone do not establish
  velocity or product fidelity between frames. Verify actual duration, cuts,
  audio policy, encoding, crop and useful still fallback. Use
  [UI/export delivery](ui-demo-delivery.md) where applicable; for narrated factual
  work retain [educational claim and timing checks](educational-explainers.md).

Higgsfield is optional: use its [production guide](../../../tools/integrations/higgsfield.md)
only when selected. No generation, paid comparison, identity training or assembly
job follows from loading this page. Keep an approved aggregate budget and resume
uncertain jobs instead of blindly resubmitting. A prompt-only pack is valid.

Return the selected master/derivatives, their current checks and any unresolved
fidelity or receiving-site limit. Generated video is not an interactive 3D scene,
a verified product demonstration or a reproducible code-rendered composition by
itself. Website integration and public deployment remain separately owned.
