## Retained and corrected

that must not dilute, semantic media/token roles, art-directed slots and comparison
against actual rendered evidence. The analytics example is a local illustration,
not a live Refero search or evidence that a design improves conversion.

Existing project/user constraints, semantic behavior and accessibility outrank a
reference. Deliberate departures are allowed and recorded. No fixed source/search
quota, required ledger/file, new interview, extra approval for authorized work or
three-option/image-generation cycle. No default-authority takeover or aesthetic bans.

contrast, scroll-lock and other craft defects therefore are not shipped here.

## Verification

From the skills checkout, set `PRIME_NATIVE_ROOT` to the actual installed Prime package:

```text
PRIME_NATIVE_ROOT=/absolute/prime-agent/package node --test tests/refero-adaptation.test.mjs
```

Checks cover scoped content/negative controls, source/hash/link integrity and native
routing. They do not measure model compliance, design quality, rendering, accessibility
or live Refero retrieval. No paid corpus is used in tests.
