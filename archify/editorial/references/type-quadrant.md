## Choose this type

Positioning along two dimensions, or four named scenarios from two drivers.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Axis meanings, direction, units or qualitative anchors, thresholds, item positions or scenario descriptions.

## Geometry and reading order

Use two crossing axes and allocate label gutters. Position marks from data, not an even distribution. A scenario variant uses named cells instead of arbitrary dots; explain the low/high combinations and any decision boundary.

## Honesty and review

Do not place every item neatly in a different quadrant without evidence. State whether scores are measured or judged. Real ties and axis-boundary values stay visible. A quadrant is not proof that the upper-right option is best; label the decision criteria.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
