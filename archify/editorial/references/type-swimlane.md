## Choose this type

Cross-functional steps and handoffs across responsible actors.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Actors/teams, owned steps, order, conditions, shared responsibilities and handoff payloads.

## Geometry and reading order

Use labeled horizontal lanes or vertical columns. Place each step inside its responsible lane and draw cross-lane handoffs explicitly. Lane population need not be equal. Reserve room for lane headings and crossing paths.

## Honesty and review

Do not treat a lane as a time axis unless time is scaled. Shared ownership must be labeled rather than hidden by straddling a boundary. Use an org chart for ownership alone; use process for repeated inputs/tools/outputs per stage.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
