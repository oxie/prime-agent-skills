CANVAS EFFECTS / ORIGINAL STUDIES

Small fields. Deliberate motion.

Four decorative studies with ordinary HTML controls. Each starts still. Play runs for eight seconds; Stop keeps the current frame. Leaving the screen stops motion without restarting it.

01 / Ripples

Mint rings cross a dark pool. During Play, move or tap the canvas to place the next ring. A bounded wave sketch, not a fluid solver.

Static description. JavaScript enables the optional preview.

02 / Particles

Amber and mint points drift across a finite field. During Play, move or tap near the points to gently push them away. There are at most 96 points.

Static description. JavaScript enables the optional preview.

03 / Glyph reveal

A moon, observatory tower, and rolling hillside emerge from a grid of text marks. This original generated scene does not capture page content or decrypt data.

Static description. JavaScript enables the optional preview.

04 / Field distortion

WebGL2 bends a striped landscape and amber sun. During Play, move or tap to shift its lens. The shader creates the entire original field; no image or network asset is used.

Static description. JavaScript enables the optional preview.
Reduced motion keeps a still preview. If canvas or WebGL2 is unavailable, the scene descriptions remain readable.
