## Deliberately not included

- Upstream director tables and director/genre prescriptions. Film associations are
  research hypotheses, not verified credit/technique facts or selection algorithms.
- `design-dna-db.txt` and `dna-index.tsv`. Review found malformed index fields,
  error-page records and motion-label contradictions; they are not a trusted
  dataset. No scraped third-party text, keyframes, images, fonts or site templates
  from that collection are bundled. This omission is not an infringement finding.
- Hash/pool selection, forced novelty, mandatory entrance/hero effect minima,
  compulsory question sets/design files and universal rejection of ordinary grids.
- The large CSS “LUT”/camera-effect catalogue. CSS inspiration is not a calibrated
  LUT, color transform or optical model. No such scientific equivalence is claimed.
- Upstream Claude/Codex/Cursor/Windsurf/Copilot/OpenAI or other platform adapters,
  global agent rules, manifest-based runtime extensions and upstream demo media.
- A dependency/CDN mandate, obscure-library preference, automatic font/asset download,
  asset-generation service, persistent mode, background loop or default server.

## Rights and verification limits

MIT on this repository does not independently license film stills, clips, logos,
fonts, website screenshots or other third-party material. Check exact source
rights and project permission before obtaining or using an asset. Source-linked
inspiration is not proof of originality, permission or non-infringement.

Pinned links establish ancestry, not the truth of every upstream film association
or code claim. The rewritten craft entries are design guidance, not benchmarked
components. The fixture's checks concern that fixture under documented conditions;
they do not establish general accessibility compliance, production backend readiness,
performance, model reliability, conversion uplift or faithful film reproduction.
