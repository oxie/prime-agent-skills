## Choose this type

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Layer names, ordering criterion, responsibilities, interfaces, gaps and cross-cutting concerns.

## Geometry and reading order

Use stacked horizontal bands with clear names and notes. A side arrow may state increasing abstraction or propagation direction. Equal heights are fine for categories; size only encodes a quantity when declared. Keep the indexing continuous or explain gaps.

## Honesty and review

Do not make unrelated groups look hierarchical. Controls need evidence of enforcement; layering alone does not prove defense in depth. For security compensation, load semantic-patterns and show escaped and final residual risk, not just solid slabs.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
