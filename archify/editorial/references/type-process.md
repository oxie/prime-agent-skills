# Process

Curated from Cathryn Lavery, diagram-design `references/type-process.md` at `3b446333f164174a571106673f943c58df282ff8` (MIT). See [retained license](../LICENSE.diagram-design). This Prime adaptation changes prescriptive styling and preserves evidence over layout.

## Choose this type

Ordered work with owners, inputs, outputs and tools visible per stage.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Stages, responsible actors, input/output payloads, tools, conditions and handoffs.

## Geometry and reading order

Use stage columns and labeled owner lanes or a consistent stage-by-slot grid. Keep repeated slots in the same order. Role badges name owners, not stage numbers. Separate IN and OUT labels consistently and connect real handoffs across cells.

## Honesty and review

Do not omit first-stage input or final output simply because there is no adjacent stage. Empty, not applicable and unknown are different. Do not truncate exact identifiers to fit a chip. Use swimlane when ownership and order suffice without the extra field structure.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
