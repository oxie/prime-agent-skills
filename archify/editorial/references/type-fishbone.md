# Fishbone / Ishikawa

Curated from Cathryn Lavery, diagram-design `references/type-fishbone.md` at `3b446333f164174a571106673f943c58df282ff8` (MIT). See [retained license](../LICENSE.diagram-design). This Prime adaptation changes prescriptive styling and preserves evidence over layout.

## Choose this type

Candidate and confirmed causes of one observed effect.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Observed effect, investigation-derived categories, concrete sub-causes and evidence/confidence status.

## Geometry and reading order

Use a horizontal spine ending at the effect box. Alternate diagonal category bones above/below (about 60 degrees), with short sub-cause ticks and clear labels. Expand both spine and viewBox when adding bones so outer tags do not clip.

## Honesty and review

A fishbone organizes investigation, not proof of causality. Multiple confirmed causes may stay visible; do not merge them to fit an accent budget. Do not fill a generic six-category template with empty labels. Use timeline for the order of failures and separate proposed fixes from the observed effect.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
