# User journey map

Curated from Cathryn Lavery, diagram-design `references/type-journey.md` at `3b446333f164174a571106673f943c58df282ff8` (MIT). See [retained license](../LICENSE.diagram-design). This Prime adaptation changes prescriptive styling and preserves evidence over layout.

## Choose this type

One persona’s actions and experience across stages, including evidenced or explicitly hypothetical sentiment.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Persona, scenario, stage order, actions, touchpoints, ordinal sentiment and research/provenance status.

## Geometry and reading order

Align stage headers over a sentiment band and repeated action/touchpoint rows. Use named ordinal levels such as high, neutral and low; place one mark per known stage. Straight segments connect observed stages without pretending to measure the intervals. Mark pain points with words and a distinct shape.

## Honesty and review

Do not fabricate sentiment or use a smooth curve to imply measurements between stages. Unknown sentiment needs a gap/label. One persona per figure keeps attribution clear. Use process for a system with no person. Synthetic journeys must be labeled hypothetical, not customer research.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
