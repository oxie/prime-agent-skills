# Symbol concepts and identity boards

Use for explicitly requested new-identity or fictional concept exploration. Keep
an existing approved name, mark and wordmark unchanged unless redesign is authorized.
Image owns the concept asset; Hallmark owns its interface application. No provider,
new intake document, fixed image count or generation step is implied by this reference.
A useful prompt-only brief is a valid deliverable, labeled as such.

## Five operations for finding a symbol

Start from the known offer and its meaning. Choose an operation that explains it;
do not combine every method just to demonstrate range. The following are original
fictional exercises for **LOANFOLD**, an imagined neighborhood tool library. The name
is an exploration label, not a claim about a real business or a cleared trademark.
These rows are alternative concepts, not five approved variants of one identity.

| Operation | How to use it | LOANFOLD exercise | What to inspect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monogram + meaning | Give an initial a useful physical or spatial action. | The foot of an L becomes a shelf supporting a short tool-handle shape; the vertical stem reads as the side of the lending cabinet. | Does L remain legible without the explanation? Remove the handle if it becomes clutter. |
| Product action | Reduce the central verb to a readable movement or relationship. | An open-top tray receives one short bent return stroke from the upper right: borrow, use, bring back. | Does it read as return to a shared place rather than a download button? Test the tray's open silhouette. |
| Metaphor fusion | Let two relevant forms share one contour instead of stacking two icons. | A book spine and a tool handle share an upright edge, joining library access with practical making. | Is the shared edge doing real visual work, or are there just two miniature pictures? |
| Negative space | Make the gap carry meaning without depending on a clever caption. | A solid tag has a shelf-shaped open notch; the absence suggests the space where a borrowed object will return. | Does the notch survive small and inverse use, or close into a dark blob? |
| Construction geometry | Build from a repeatable primitive that fits the meaning. | Equal rectangular modules form an open cabinet corner; one detached module suggests an item away on loan. | Can a designer reproduce the module relationship in an actual drawing? Do not invent circles or ratios after the fact. |

Geometry in a concept board is a proposed explanation, not proof of a measured
construction. If dimensions are not defined in a real source drawing, show a plain
silhouette or labeled parts, not impressive but fictional technical guides.

For campaign meaning, environment and human ritual, use [brand worlds](brand-worlds.md).
That method supplies the scene; these operations supply the mark logic. For example,
people returning tools at a shared counter can express LOANFOLD's meaning without
turning its mark into a photograph of tools or inventing satisfied-customer claims.

## Optional board layouts: panels have jobs

Use a board only when comparing applications will help the decision. A single sheet,
a few selected panels or a custom layout may be better; 3×3 and 2×3 are alternatives,
not quotas. Vary visual weight so the cover can be quiet, the application functional
and the image panel atmospheric. Keep the same chosen mark across the whole board.

### 3×3 — broad identity exploration (row-major order)

| Position | Panel purpose | Concrete content for the chosen return-tray concept |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identity cover | One large return-tray mark and the exact name LOANFOLD; ample clear space. |
| 2 | Symbol logic | The same silhouette with tray and incoming stroke labeled; no invented measurement grid. |
| 3 | Digital use | A conceptual catalog header using the mark at a smaller size; no fake working controls or real product evidence. |
| 4 | Essence | The proposed line “Tools to share. A place to return.”, clearly concept copy. |
| 5 | Palette roles | Paper, ink and selected accent mapped to the host's approved tokens; exact values only when supplied or explicitly proposed. |
| 6 | Type behavior | LOANFOLD plus a readable label sample in available approved type; no claim to supply a font. |
| 7 | Physical use | The same mark on an imagined lending tag, labeled a concept mockup. |
| 8 | Image direction | A return-counter scene brief from brand-worlds, or an approved image; distinguish prompt from pixels. |
| 9 | System detail | One-color and inverse appearances, or a crop showing clear space; no decorative certification seals. |

### 2×3 — compact application story (two columns, three rows)

| Position | Panel purpose | Concrete content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mark and name | Quiet cover with the same return-tray mark and LOANFOLD. |
| 2 | Product surface | Concept catalog header showing the mark in context, not a product screenshot. |
| 3 | Functional detail | A proposed item-label treatment reading “Return location”; not a fabricated command, status or successful transaction. |
| 4 | Campaign atmosphere | The shared-counter scene, or a clearly labeled prompt-only scene description. |
| 5 | Symbol inspection | Same silhouette at small size and in one color; annotate observed loss, not invented construction precision. |
| 6 | Promise and voice | The same proposed line with a short label sample; leave room to judge tone. |

## Worked concept prompt / board brief

This example chooses the **product-action** operation only. It describes a board;
it is not an image-generation result or an instruction to invoke a provider.

```text
Purpose: fictional LOANFOLD identity exploration for a neighborhood tool library.
Meaning: shared access and the act of returning tools to a common place.
Chosen mark: one open-top tray with one short bent return stroke entering from the
upper right. Preserve that silhouette, direction and relationship in every panel.
Do not substitute the L/shelf, book/handle or notched-tag alternatives.
Name: LOANFOLD, spelled exactly. Proposed line: Tools to share. A place to return.
Board: optional 2×3, two columns by three rows. Use the six panel purposes above.
Composition: generous cover space; smaller mark in the catalog header; compact
item-label detail; wider counter-scene crop; plain small-size symbol comparison;
quiet closing line. Keep equal gutters, not equal visual loudness.
Surface: use the approved host paper, ink and accent roles and available typography.
If no values or font are supplied, leave them as role proposals rather than invent
an official palette or named licensed font. Keep text editable outside concept art.
Scene: an imagined tool return at a shared counter, clearly staged concept imagery;
no customer names, metrics, endorsement logos, official seals or documentary claims.
Consistency: if an approved source mark exists, place that asset in the board rather
than ask for a new version in each panel. Without one, label the repeated mark a
provisional drawing and inspect differences; consistency is not guaranteed by words.
Deliverable boundary: concept brief/board only, not a vector master, font package,
production brand manual or clearance opinion. No automatic generation or publication.
```

## Review the decision, then separate production work

Judge whether the symbol communicates the intended action, stays consistent and
works at the named applications' actual size. If a real source mark exists, inspect
16/32px, one-color and inverse renderings; a tiny panel on a board does not prove
favicon legibility. Simplify a stroke that vanishes before changing the core meaning.
Compare exact name/copy and mark orientation across every occurrence. Label proposed
colors separately from approved tokens; rendered swatches alone do not establish
exact values or print spot-color specifications.

A raster board cannot deliver editable vectors, reliable geometry, licensed fonts,
master lockups or legal clearance. A vector-shaped image is not necessarily an SVG;
an SVG container is not proof of suitable logo paths. Keep the approved master intact.
Production drawing, font licensing, application checks and appropriate trademark or
similarity review are separate work when requested. Do not promise an “ownable” mark
from appearance. Report what exists: prompt, board image or actual source artwork,
which outputs were inspected, and which production questions remain open.
