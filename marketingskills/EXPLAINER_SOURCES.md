## Primary evidence and local decisions

- [W3C description of visual information](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/description/):
  plan access to essential visual meaning, including integrated description. The
  local guide does not ban narration of diagrams or claim transcripts replace all
  media accommodations.
- [W3C WCAG 2.3.1 explanation](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html):
  flash risk is not a count of permitted glitch components. Final viewing conditions
  and applicable thresholds matter; no compliance claim follows from our text tests.
- Existing Video UI-demo guidance supplies export and seekability checks. Archify
  supplies semantic diagram guidance; Tutor distinguishes explanation from demonstrated
  learning. The new reference links those responsibilities without adding a renderer.

Claim-to-passage mapping, illustrative-data labels, shared revision/timing identity
and dependent-output invalidation are original local responses to review findings.
They are not claims that a source URL proves truth or that an animated explanation
proves learning. No HNSW hop-count example or universal RAG threshold is imported.

## Exclusions and evidence limits

Do not import the fixed black/purple style, glow/motion quotas, four mandatory stops,
worker-count/time promises, cloud/local TTS defaults, global temp cleanup, scaffold,
QC scripts, automatic lessons edits or an additional explainer skill. The reviewed
upstream executable defects are not repaired or endorsed by this documentation.

Source identities and current local hashes are in explainer-provenance.json. Earlier
marketing/Huashu pins, licenses, metadata and experimental records stay historical;
strict test adapters reconstruct only the exact approved predecessor bytes.
Validation covers source contracts, links, packaging and native discovery. No
upstream execution, rendered video, font/media test, model experiment, TTS call or
accessibility certification was performed for this integration.
