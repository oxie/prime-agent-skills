# Org chart / responsibility map

Curated from Cathryn Lavery, diagram-design `references/type-org-chart.md` at `3b446333f164174a571106673f943c58df282ff8` (MIT). See [retained license](../LICENSE.diagram-design). This Prime adaptation changes prescriptive styling and preserves evidence over layout.

## Choose this type

Accountability, reporting, escalation or work routing among people, teams or agents.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Owners, role scope, actual reporting/routing relationships, contact route when supplied, vacancies and unavailable owners.

## Geometry and reading order

Place an accountable root/front door above primary groups and specialists. Separate reporting lines from dotted-line responsibility or escalation using labels and a legend. Put approval/escalation rules beside the hierarchy, not as fictitious employees.

## Honesty and review

Do not create extra managers merely to balance a tree. Show vacant, inactive and unwired roles explicitly. Reporting, approval and invocation are different relations. Use swimlane when process order rather than ownership is the question.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
