## Choose this type

Ranked hierarchy, foundation-to-apex relationships, or shrinking conversion cohorts.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Orientation, hierarchy meaning or measured stage counts, cohort definition, denominators and stage order.

## Geometry and reading order

Pyramid points up with a broad foundation; funnel narrows toward the outcome. Label every layer and put long notes outside with leaders. A conceptual pyramid says width is schematic. Quantitative funnels must declare whether width or area carries count and compute it consistently.

## Honesty and review

No invented uniform drop-off. Report conversion denominators (prior stage versus initial cohort). Zero and missing are distinct. Do not force increasing measured stages into a narrowing shape; explain re-entry or use bars. Use one orientation per figure.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
