## Choose this type

The current or legacy landscape, including manual handoffs and known friction.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Snapshot date, systems, departments or phases, actual file/API handoffs, evidence-backed pain points.

## Geometry and reading order

Lay zones left to right or top to bottom; arrange components within their real zone. Label handoffs with formats or actions such as CSV, email or manual copy. Use a separate treatment for external/out-of-scope systems. Place cross-cutting notes below the map.

## Honesty and review

Do not insert the proposed platform into the current-state picture. Separate observed pain from hypothesis. Preserve backward handoffs and missing integrations; label unknowns rather than making the old system look artificially broken.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
