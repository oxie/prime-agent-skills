# Scope and evidence

All entities, policies, producer names, dates and revision labels in this bundle
are fictional. No computation, database query or human verification is claimed.
It demonstrates an export for a reporting analyst who needs the applicable meaning
of Active Workspace. It does not define billing, access control or customer health.

The source owner is the fictional reporting team. In this example, the
[counting policy](policies/activity.md) at `policy-r2` is the source of truth for
the [current definition](definitions/active-workspace.md). In a real project, keep
the accepted policy at its existing owner; export only authorized content.

`example_revision` and `source_revision` are local illustrative extensions, not
OKF fields that prove identity or integrity. Bind a real handoff to actual source
and delivered-file revisions through the project's existing evidence record.
There is no `verified` field because this example records no real source review.
The producer/time and expiry on the current definition are illustrative metadata,
not evidence of when an actual team wrote or approved a policy.

Only the four concepts linked through this index are delivered. Event data,
execution receipts and historical report outputs are not included. Do not infer
that an actual workspace qualifies from the presence of a definition alone.
Read the old definition for reports before the effective instant; replacement is
not permission to rewrite historical results. No public hosting or external
service is needed to inspect these files.
