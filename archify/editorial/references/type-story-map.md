# User story map

Curated from Cathryn Lavery, diagram-design `references/type-story-map.md` at `3b446333f164174a571106673f943c58df282ff8` (MIT). See [retained license](../LICENSE.diagram-design). This Prime adaptation changes prescriptive styling and preserves evidence over layout.

## Choose this type

A narrative backbone divided into release scope, not a status board.

This is a selectively loaded authoring brief, not a new deterministic renderer. Follow [design](design.md); typed Archify contracts remain authoritative in typed mode.

## Evidence to collect

Ordered user activities and steps, user-visible stories, release slices, explicit release cut and known risks.

## Geometry and reading order

Columns follow narrative order; rows below the backbone are release slices. Align each story under its activity, even where other cells are empty. Draw and label a release cut below the selected slice. Keep any highest-risk story’s evidence beside its marker.

## Honesty and review

Columns are not Todo/Doing/Done and rows are not sentiment. A gap may mean no story selected, not unknown work completed. Do not invent a riskiest story to satisfy a focal rule. Preserve necessary nonfunctional work in notes/backlog scope rather than pretending it is a user action.

Check text/connector clearance at the actual output size. Preserve required facts when splitting a dense view; name omissions and cross-view relations. Static lint does not prove semantic or geometric correctness.
